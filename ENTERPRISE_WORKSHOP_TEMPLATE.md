# Enterprise Workshop Template

A structured, single-session template for running `DISCOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md`'s conversation as one real workshop rather than several loosely-scoped calls. PROPOSED, unvalidated by a real pilot.

## Who should be in the room

The prospect's named technical owner and named business owner (`PILOT_PROGRAM_GUIDE.md`'s Qualification stage should have already identified both), together, in the same session. Running Discovery separately with each misses the exact mismatches (Question 2 in `DISCOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md`, whether stated policy matches documented policy) that only surface when both perspectives are in the same room at once.

## Agenda (90 minutes, PROPOSED timing)

**0-10 min, framing**: what Runtime Authority is, in the language of `ENTERPRISE_MESSAGING_GUIDE.md`, pitched specifically at this audience (a business owner needs the Runtime Authority Guide's framing more than the Technical Overview's).

**10-40 min, the real document**: walk through the prospect's own uploaded authority document together, live. Ask `DISCOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md`'s Questions 2 and 3 directly against specific passages, not in the abstract. This is the highest-value 30 minutes of the entire pilot process; do not rush it to protect time for the rest of the agenda.

**40-55 min, the action and the integration path**: `DISCOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md`'s Questions 1 and 4, with the technical owner leading.

**55-70 min, live demonstration**: run a real Intent through the platform live, using a scenario constructed from what was just discussed, not a generic canned demo. Show all three possible outcomes conceptually (ALLOW, DENY, HUMAN_REVIEW), and show a real Evidence record's independent verification, live, since this is the platform's most concretely provable claim and the workshop is the best possible moment to prove it in front of both stakeholders at once.

**70-85 min, success criteria**: `DISCOVERY_PLAYBOOK.md`'s Question 6, written down in the room, agreed by both stakeholders before anyone leaves.

**85-90 min, next steps**: confirm the Deployment stage's first concrete action and who owns it.

## What comes out of this workshop

Exactly the Discovery document `PILOT_PROGRAM_GUIDE.md` already specifies: the specific action, the specific document, the specific success metrics, and an explicit non-goal list. This workshop's whole purpose is producing that document in one structured session instead of assembling it piecemeal across several unstructured calls.

## What this template deliberately avoids

A generic "AI demo" agenda unrelated to the prospect's own real document and real action. Every enterprise workshop template that leads with a slide deck instead of the customer's own material produces a worse Discovery document; this template is built specifically to avoid that failure mode.
